Inside the recruiting engine

What happens between “we need someone” and day one

Recruiting is the product. Here's the machine that turns a role description into a shortlist in 7–10 business days — and an onboarded, certified hire in about 3 weeks.

Intake & calibration

Day 0

A recruiter who knows your role family scopes the work with you: stack, hours, seniority, and what great actually looks like. We calibrate on real profiles before sourcing starts.

Sourcing & screening

Days 1–5

We source across four markets — network, referrals, and a decade of placement data — then screen for skills, written and spoken English, and an AI-fluency baseline. Most applicants do not pass.

Assessment & shortlist

Days 5–10

Role-specific assessments, not trivia: a bookkeeper reconciles, a developer ships, a CSR handles a live scenario. You get a shortlist of 2–4 with our notes, in 7–10 business days.

Interview, offer, onboard

Weeks 2–3

You interview finalists; we handle offer, employment, equipment shipping, and security setup. Certification starts before day one. Live in about 3 weeks.

The same engine is subscribable on its own — see Recruiting as a service line if you employ directly.

Engagement models

Fully Managed or Co-Managed — your call

Fully Managed

A DOXA team lead runs daily supervision, QA and coaching. You set direction and review outcomes. Best for first offshore teams and volume functions like support and billing.

Co-Managed

Your manager owns the day-to-day; DOXA owns employment, HR, payroll, equipment and retention. Best when the role embeds in an existing team — accounting, development, marketing.

DOXA Talent's annual team-member turnover is 14%, against a 30–40% BPO industry norm. That's the retention your ramp-up investment is protected by.

Process FAQ

What buyers ask about the process

How long does it take to hire offshore staff?
With DOXA: first shortlist in about 10 business days, and most roles live in 30–45 days — including onboarding, equipment provisioning, and systems access.
Do I manage the person day to day?
You direct the work; DOXA runs the employment. In Fully Managed, a DOXA team lead also handles daily supervision and coaching. In Co-Managed, your manager runs the day-to-day and DOXA handles everything behind the seat.
Do I get to interview candidates?
Always. You interview a curated shortlist and make the final call — DOXA screens for skills, English, and culture fit before anyone reaches you.
