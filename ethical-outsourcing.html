The buyer's checklist

Six questions to ask any outsourcing provider

Take this list into every vendor conversation — including ours. Ethical providers answer all six in writing without flinching.

1

Are they employees or contractors?

Misclassification shifts risk onto workers and you. DOXA VIPs are 100% direct employees.

2

Is pay above local market?

Below-market pay guarantees churn. DOXA pays above-market with annual reviews.

3

Do benefits actually exist?

Health coverage, paid leave, government contributions — in writing. Standard for every VIP.

4

What is the real turnover number?

Ask for it. DOXA's is 14% annually; the BPO norm is 30–40%.

5

Is there third-party verification?

Great Place to Work-Certified™ — measured from employee surveys, not marketing.

6

Who owns the equipment and security?

Company-issued encrypted devices under SOC 2 Type II controls — not personal laptops.
