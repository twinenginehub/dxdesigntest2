About DOXA

We hit the wall ourselves. Then we built the way through.

DOXA's founders hit the talent wall in 2020: rising wages, scarce people, constrained growth. They tried every outsourcing option and kept finding the same problems — hidden fees, misclassified workers, teams that disappeared. So they built the model where everyone wins. 1,000+ team members later, it's your turn.
