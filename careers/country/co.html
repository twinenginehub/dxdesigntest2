Life at DOXA

Let's grow — together.

DOXA was founded in 2020 to prove outsourcing could be done with dignity: real employment, real benefits, real careers — never misclassified contractors. It worked. Today more than 1,000+ VIPs across the Philippines, Colombia, Vietnam, and Kenya build careers with US companies that treat them as teammates, not tickets.

The next chapter raises the bar again: every VIP becomes a certified AI professional — trained in workflow design and the skills that make you more valuable as AI matures, funded by DOXA. Your growth is the product. We mean that economically.

Candidate FAQ

Asked before applying

Does DOXA provide equipment?
Yes. Every VIP receives a company-issued laptop, monitor, headset, and all necessary software — delivered before your first day.
Is the work fully remote?
Yes. DOXA is remote-first. You work from home, with the equipment, security setup, and support to do it well.
What kinds of companies would I work with?
Growth-stage US businesses across finance, healthcare, IT, legal, and professional services — one dedicated client, not a rotating queue.
What benefits do VIPs receive?
Above-market salaries, comprehensive healthcare, paid time off, meal subsidies, funded AI certification, and structured career development.
What roles does DOXA hire for?
Nine families: finance & accounting, assistants, customer support & sales, marketing, IT, HR, legal, healthcare, and ISP/MSP — from junior specialists to controllers and senior engineers.
Do people actually stay?
Voluntary turnover is 14% — less than half the industry norm — and our employee NPS is 72. The majority of VIPs have been with us for years.
