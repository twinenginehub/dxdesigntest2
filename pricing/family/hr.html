Pricing FAQ

No surprises. That's the point.

What does the monthly rate include?
Everything: the professional's salary and full benefits, recruiting, HR management, payroll, legal compliance, company-issued encrypted equipment, and ongoing AI training. One rate, no line items.
Are there setup fees or recruitment charges?
No. There are no setup fees, no recruitment charges, and no hidden costs, and no fees at all until your hire starts.
What is the cancellation policy?
30 days, in writing, any time. No long-term lock-in.
Why do rates vary within a range?
Scope, seniority within level, specialized tooling, and country of hire. The published band is the honest range across the DOXA network; your exact quote comes with the shortlist.
Is a US-hours schedule extra?
No. US business-hours coverage is standard, night shift is normal practice in the Philippines, and Colombia overlaps US time zones natively.
