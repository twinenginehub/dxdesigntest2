Straight answers

The questions every buyer asks

DOXA Talent charges one all-inclusive monthly rate per role — no setup fees, no recruitment charges, no hidden costs — with a 30-day cancellation policy.

How much does offshore staffing cost with DOXA?
One all-inclusive monthly rate per role — typically $1,200–$3,600 depending on function and seniority, which is up to 70% less than a fully-loaded US equivalent. No setup fees, no recruitment charges, no hidden costs, and a 30-day cancellation policy.
Will my offshore team work my business hours?
Yes. Philippine teams work US night shift as standard practice, and Colombia overlaps US time zones natively. You choose the schedule; overlap is guaranteed in writing.
How good is the English?
Professional working English is a screening gate, not a hope. Every candidate is assessed on written and spoken English before you ever meet them, and you interview finalists yourself before any hire.
How is DOXA different from a BPO or a VA marketplace?
DOXA VIPs are full-time direct employees dedicated to one client — not pooled agents or fractional contractors. That model produces 14% annual turnover against a 30–40% BPO norm, so your institutional knowledge compounds instead of walking out the door.
What about data security?
SOC 2 Type II audited controls, company-issued encrypted laptops, two-factor authentication, and no personal-device access. Security posture that passes enterprise and regulated-industry review.
What if the person doesn't work out?
We replace at no additional recruitment cost, and the 30-day cancellation policy means you're never locked into a seat that isn't working. In practice, replacement is rare — retention is the model.
Won't AI just replace these roles?
AI is why the model works. Every DOXA professional is trained on the AI tools reshaping their function, so you're hiring people who multiply the technology — the combination outperforms either one alone. That's the Engineered Workforce.
How fast can I hire?
First shortlist in 7–10 business days; an onboarded hire in about 3 weeks. Speed comes from a recruiting operation that already employs 1,000+ professionals across four countries.
